## Preference review (Jev)

After every task, run:

  npx jev-pref review

- Exit 1 (gate violated) ΓåÆ fix the flagged prefs and re-run (max 3 times, then escalate).
- Exit 0 with advisory notes ΓåÆ address or explicitly note why not.
- Exit 0 clean ΓåÆ continue.
- Exit 2 (config/infra error) ΓåÆ fix setup; never treat as approval.
- Keys come from the environment (`JEV_API_KEY`/`TYPESAFE_API_KEY`/`AI_GATEWAY_API_KEY`); never commit them.
