# jev-pref

When setting up jev-pref, run:

    npx jev-pref setup

Follow the instructions printed by the command. They are the authoritative,
repository-aware setup protocol.

For an already configured repository, follow its local `CLAUDE.md`,
`AGENTS.md`, or other agent instructions and the policy in `jev-pref.json`.
